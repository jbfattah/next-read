How scoring works
  • Favorite author: +3 to +10 based on how many of their books you've loved (4-5★).
  • Series continuation: +4 to +9 if the series has loved entries on your read shelf.
  • Early in loved series: +1 bonus for books 1–3 of a series you already love.
  • Recency-of-add nudge: +0.5 to +1 if you shelved it recently (intent signal).
  • Popularity: ±1–3 tiebreak from Goodreads ratings count + average (off here — Goodreads dropped that column).
  • Author burnout: −3 if you've rated 2+ books by them low AND more low than high.
  • Hated-subject evidence: −4 if title matches a known-disliked theme.
  • Freshness multiplier: 1.0× (≤4y old) → 0.85× (5–9y) → 0.7× (10–19y) → 0.55× (unknown / older).
  • Diversity cap: only 1 book per author kept in the final top list.
